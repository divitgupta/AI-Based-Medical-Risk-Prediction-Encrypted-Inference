    Data quality

    Corrections applied before training

    1 · The CSV's labels are inverted

    In this Kaggle export target = 1 means no disease. Every clinical sign points the same way, and 165 ones matches the 164 absence cases of the original UCI data. We use disease = 1 − target, so recall means sensitivity to disease.

    2 · Invalid category codes

    Two codes do not exist in the UCI codebook — they are how this export stored missing values. They are treated as missing and imputed (most frequent value) inside each training fold, keeping all 303 records.
