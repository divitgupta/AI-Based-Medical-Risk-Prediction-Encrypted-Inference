Privacy-preserving diagnostics

AI-Based Medical Risk Prediction & Encrypted Inference

Three models of increasing complexity compared with 5-fold cross-validation on Heart Disease — each one also served under Fully Homomorphic Encryption, so the server never sees the patient's data.

Dataset
Working prototype · Phase 1 + 2 Heart Disease · 5-fold CV

Research questions

How does diagnostic performance change with model complexity — and can the prediction be made without the server ever seeing the patient's data?
